    Interaction study. Nothing is persisted and nothing is uploaded: files stay in this tab only, the progress bar is simulated, and reloading restores the samples. It shows attaching files to an initiative, an epic, or a story — drag and drop, the file picker, paste, what an upload looks like while it runs, and the four reasons a file is turned away. Open it with ?itemId=init-planning, ?itemId=epic-portal, ?itemId=story-101, or ?itemId=story-102&empty for the first-file state. Attachments sit outside the requirements baseline today — L1.md §5 excludes them — so every behaviour here is a proposal. The study assumes one attachment list per work item, no inheritance down the hierarchy (an epic does not show its initiative’s files), and, since L1-013 establishes no individual identity, the uploader is recorded as the assistant named at the time rather than a signed-in person.
