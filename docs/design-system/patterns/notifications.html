Pattern

Notifications

Toasts and banners: priority, grouping and dismissal.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

  • States
  • Responsive
  • Accessibility

Overview

  • No notification centre today; toasts and banners cover every case in the mocks.

When to use

  • Toast: result of the person's own action.
  • Banner: state of the workspace (shared passcode, sprint ending).
  • Inline alert: state of one form or panel.

Structure

Sprint 2 ends in 2 days.
Time logged.
Banner on top, toast bottom-right.

States

  • Up to three toasts stack; older ones leave first.
  • Success auto-dismisses after 5s, paused on hover/focus; errors persist.

Responsive

  • Toasts span the bottom on phones.

Accessibility

  • Region exists at load; polite for status, assertive for errors only.

Content

  • Lead with the object: "QBC-269 moved to Done."
