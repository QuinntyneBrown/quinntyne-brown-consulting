Shape

Five radii and full; radius grows with the element.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview

Overview

  • The mocks used 12 radii (6–20px); they collapse to five.
  • Small things are tighter, big things rounder.

Usage

  • xs 4px: checkbox, focus corners.
  • sm 6px: tags, points chip, tooltip.
  • md 8px: buttons, inputs, nav items, menus.
  • lg 12px: cards, story cards, columns, tables.
  • xl 16px: dialogs.
  • full: pills, avatars, counts, hours.
