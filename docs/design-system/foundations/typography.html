Typography

Inter at a dense 15px body, a nine-step scale from 12 to 42px, and four weights taken from the mocks.

Overview

  • Family: Inter with system fallbacks; mono for keys, passcode digits and references.
  • Body is 15px (--font-size-md): the workboard is a dense tool and the mocks set it everywhere.
  • Nothing below 12px; the mocks' 10–11px labels were raised (drift D5).

Usage

  • Page title: --text-display at a fluid 30–42px (clamp).
  • Section and dialog titles: --text-h2 (22px).
  • Card and story titles: --text-h4 at 15–16px.
  • Labels, pills: --text-label; keys and eyebrows: --text-overline uppercase with --letter-spacing-wide.
  • Prose max width: --layout-measure (72ch).
  • Numbers in tables and chips use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.

Accessibility

  • Text resizes to 200% without loss: sizes are in rem.
  • Line height ≥ 1.4 for body; paragraph spacing ≥ 1.5× via margins.
  • Uppercase only for short overlines; never for sentences.
