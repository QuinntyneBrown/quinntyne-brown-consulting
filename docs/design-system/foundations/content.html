How the workboard speaks: plain, short, sentence case, verbs on buttons.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Casing and verbs
  • Numbers, dates, times
  • Error formula
  • Empty-state formula
  • Microcopy library

Overview

  • Voice: a calm colleague. Tone tightens for errors and destructive actions.

Casing and verbs

  • Sentence case everywhere: "New story", "Start sprint".
  • Buttons start with a verb; destructive buttons name the object.
  • Status words: To do, In progress, Done, Ready, Draft, Archived.

Numbers, dates, times

  • Dates: "Oct 16"; ranges "Oct 16 – Oct 29" with an en dash.
  • Hours: two decimals in lists ("2.50 h").
  • Counts: digits ("46 stories").
  • Story keys: "QBC-269".

Error formula

  • What happened + what to do: "The sprint wasn't started. Complete Sprint 1 first."
  • No codes, no blame, no exclamation marks.

Empty-state formula

  • Title: "No … yet" or "No … match".
  • One sentence with the next step; one action.
