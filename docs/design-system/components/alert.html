Alert

An inline message that stays: a form failure, a sprint ending, a locked workspace.

Overview

  • Inline alerts sit next to what they describe.
  • Banners span the top of a page for workspace-wide states.
  • role="alert" only when the alert appears in response to something.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone.
  2. TitleBold, one line.
  3. BodyOne or two sentences.
  4. ActionsLinks or small buttons.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed statically; product code gets them from real pseudo-classes and attributes.

Static; dismissible alerts add a ghost close button at the end.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its container; actions wrap.

Theming

Component tokens: --alert-fg, --alert-bg, --alert-border, --alert-icon. Each aliases a semantic token, so a theme re-colours the component by overriding semantic tokens and a product re-skin overrides the component tokens; neither edits the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Static alerts are plain content; alerts injected after an action use role="alert" (assertive) or role="status" (polite).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

Alerts don't take focus, except the form error summary.

Labelling

Icons are decorative; the title carries the meaning.

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions over --duration-fast, which the tokens zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Controls are at least --control-height-md (40px) tall, above the 24px minimum; in dense rows the whole row is also a target and neighbours keep 8px apart.

Content

  • Title says what happened; body says what to do.
