Tooltip

A short text label that appears on hover and focus, mostly for icon-only buttons.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole=tooltip

Overview

  • Names or briefly explains; never holds links or buttons.
  • Shows after 300ms on hover, immediately on focus; hides on Esc.

Anatomy

  1. BubbleInverse colours, --radius-sm.
  2. Shortcut (optional)Mono.

Variants

Move to In progressNew storyN
Text; with shortcut.
HTML
<span class="tooltip" role="tooltip">Move to In progress</span><span class="tooltip" role="tooltip">New story<kbd>N</kbd></span>

Sizes

Not applicable: One size; max 256px wide.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed statically; product code gets them from real pseudo-classes and attributes.

Visible or hidden; it has no interactive states.

Responsive behaviour

  • On touch, tooltips do not show; icon buttons rely on aria-label and familiar icons.

Theming

The component reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens). The dark theme re-maps those tokens; nothing in the component changes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="tooltip" referenced by aria-describedby (or it is the aria-label text).

Keyboard

KeyAction
FocusShows.
EscHides without moving focus.

Focus

Tooltip never takes focus.

Labelling

Its text matches the button's accessible name.

Contrast

Tooltip text

Motion

Fades in over --duration-fast; out instantly.

Touch

Not available on touch; never put essential information only in a tooltip.

Content

  • Two to four words; no period.
