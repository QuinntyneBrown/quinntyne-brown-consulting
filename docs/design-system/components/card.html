Card

A bordered container for one thing: an assistant, an initiative, a sprint hero.

Overview

  • Use to group content about one object.
  • A clickable card has one real link (the title); the whole card is the hit area through a pseudo-element.
  • Board stories use the denser story card.

Anatomy

  1. HeadIdentity and status.
  2. BodyTags or summary.
  3. FootMetric and link, hairline above.

Responsive behaviour

  • Assistant cards sit in .grid--auto (min 16rem), giving 1–4 columns.
  • The sprint hero stacks its meta under the title below 760px.

Theming

Component tokens: --card-padding. Each aliases a semantic token, so a theme re-colours the component by overriding semantic tokens and a product re-skin overrides the component tokens; neither edits the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<article> with a heading; interactive cards contain one link and the card is not itself focusable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterFollows the title link.

Focus

Focus on the title link draws the ring around the whole card (:has(:focus-visible)).

Labelling

The heading names the card.

Title
Body

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions over --duration-fast, which the tokens zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Controls are at least --control-height-md (40px) tall, above the 24px minimum; in dense rows the whole row is also a target and neighbours keep 8px apart.

Content

  • Title is the object's name; body is one line.
