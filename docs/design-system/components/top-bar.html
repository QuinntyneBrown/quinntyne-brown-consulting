Top bar

The bar above every page: breadcrumb on the left, page actions on the right, menu button on phones.

Overview

  • Sticky; one per page.
  • Holds the breadcrumb and up to two actions; the page head holds the rest.

Anatomy

  1. BreadcrumbWhere you are.
  2. ActionsPrimary page action.
  3. Bar80px, hairline bottom border.

Sizes

Not applicable: Two heights: 80px default, 72px compact.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed statically; product code gets them from real pseudo-classes and attributes.

The bar has no interactive states of its own; its buttons and links carry theirs.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 760px: compact height, the breadcrumb hides, the menu button appears and opens the sidebar drawer.
  • Side padding follows --layout-margin.

Theming

The component reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens). The dark theme re-maps those tokens; nothing in the component changes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header> (banner landmark) containing the breadcrumb <nav>. The menu button has aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the sidebar.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width solid --color-focus-ring, --focus-ring-offset gap) appears on :focus-visible only. The mocks drew a 25% green halo (rgba(39,103,73,.25)) that measured 1.3:1; the system ring is solid and passes 3:1 (drift D2).

Labelling

Icon-only actions are named ("New story").

Contrast

Title
Breadcrumb

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions over --duration-fast, which the tokens zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Controls are at least --control-height-md (40px) tall, above the 24px minimum; in dense rows the whole row is also a target and neighbours keep 8px apart.

Content

  • The compact title is the page name, not the product.
