Story card

A story on the sprint board: key, title, epic context, points and assignee.

Overview

  • Only on the board columns.
  • Drag between columns, or move with its card actions (keyboard and touch alternative).
  • Opens the story dialog from its title.

Anatomy

  1. KeyOverline, subtle.
  2. StatusBadge, optional.
  3. TitleLink that opens the story.
  4. ContextInitiative · epic, one line, ellipsis.
  5. PointsEstimate chip.
  6. AssigneeAvatar.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill the column; below 1000px the board stacks columns and cards go full width.

Theming

The component reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens). The dark theme re-maps those tokens; nothing in the component changes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<article aria-labelledby>; the title is a link. Drag and drop has a keyboard alternative: the card's actions ("Move to In progress") or the story dialog's status field.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOn the title: opens the story.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width solid --color-focus-ring, --focus-ring-offset gap) appears on :focus-visible only. The mocks drew a 25% green halo (rgba(39,103,73,.25)) that measured 1.3:1; the system ring is solid and passes 3:1 (drift D2).

Labelling

Points chip has aria-label="8 points"; the avatar is decorative next to the assignee name in the dialog, so the card adds aria-label on the avatar ("Assigned to Vanessa") in product code.

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions over --duration-fast, which the tokens zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

The title link and the action buttons are the targets; dragging needs a long-press on touch.

Content

  • Titles are verb-first tasks, sentence case.
  • Context is "Initiative · Epic".
