# Code of Conduct

QBC Workboard adopts the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This document states the project's participation and enforcement expectations.

## Commitment

Project participants are expected to help create an open, welcoming, inclusive,
and professional community. Participation should remain free from harassment
and discrimination regardless of identity, background, ability, experience, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect and empathy.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and trade-offs without attacking individuals.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the outcome.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's
  information.
- Prioritize the health of the project and its community over winning an
  argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, derogatory comments, trolling, or personal and political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions or maintainer work.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commits, project
communication, community events, and public representation of QBC Workboard.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[`quinntynebrown@gmail.com`](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com) with the subject
`[CONDUCT] QBC Workboard`. Include relevant links, dates, context, and the names
of involved participants. Do not include the report in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Information
will be shared only with people required to investigate and resolve the matter.
Conflicts of interest should be identified in the report so an alternative
reviewer may be considered.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, issue private warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a participant. The response will consider the impact, severity,
context, repetition, and safety of affected people.

Retaliation, repeated violations, and failure to respect an enforcement boundary
may result in a stronger response. Maintainers may explain moderation decisions
when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.
