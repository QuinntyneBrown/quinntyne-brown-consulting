A labelled multi-line text field for descriptions, acceptance criteria and notes. `qbc-textarea` renders a `<label>`, a vertically resizable `<textarea>` and an optional hint, and works with reactive forms or with the `value` input and `valueChange` output.

Inputs: `label`, `placeholder`, `hint`, `required` (adds an asterisk), `readonly`, `disabled`, `full` (spans a whole form-grid row) and `ariaLabel`.
