## Best practices

### Layout

- Stack cards vertically on the Initiatives page; each card is full width.
- On narrow screens the header stacks and the actions wrap below the copy.

### Content

- Keep `description` to one sentence that states the outcome.
- Use `summary` for counts ("3 epics · 14 stories · 42 story points"), not prose.
- Limit header actions to one or two small buttons.

### Accessibility

- The title is an `h2`; place cards under a page-level `h1` such as `qbc-page-header`.
