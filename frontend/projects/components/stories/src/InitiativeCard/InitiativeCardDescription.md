A bordered card for one initiative on the Initiatives page. `qbc-initiative-card` renders a raised header with the `title` (default "Untitled initiative"), a `description` and a short `summary` line such as the epic, story and story-point counts.

Elements marked with the `actions` attribute project into the header beside the title; everything else projects into the body, which is indented to sit under the title and usually holds the initiative's epics.
