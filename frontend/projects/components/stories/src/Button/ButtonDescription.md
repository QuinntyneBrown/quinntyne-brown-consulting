The standard action button. `qbc-button` wraps a native `button` and projects its label.

`variant` is `primary` (default), `secondary`, `quiet` or `danger`; `size` is `md` (default), `sm` or `xs`; `type` is `button` (default), `submit` or `reset`. `full` stretches it to the container width and `disabled` disables it. `ariaLabel` and `ariaExpanded` set the matching ARIA attributes for icon-only or disclosure buttons.
