A labelled single-line text field. `qbc-text-input` renders a `<label>`, an `<input>` and an optional hint, and works with both reactive forms (`formControl`/`formControlName`) and the `value` input plus the `valueChange` output.

`type` is `text` (default), `email`, `password`, `number`, `date` or `search`; `size` is `md` (default) or `sm`. Other inputs: `placeholder`, `hint`, `required` (adds an asterisk), `readonly`, `disabled`, `full` (spans a whole form-grid row), `labelHidden`, `icon`, `ariaLabel`, `autocomplete`, and `min`/`step` for number fields.
