A thin horizontal bar showing how much of a sprint, epic or initiative is complete. `qbc-progress` fills its accent bar to `value` percent, clamped to 0–100.

It renders `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` and an `aria-label` from `label`. Set `mini` for a fixed 110px, slightly thinner track used inline on rows; otherwise the bar fills its container.
