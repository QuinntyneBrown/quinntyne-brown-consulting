## Best practices

### Layout

- Group nav items inside a `<nav>` with an `aria-label`, as `qbc-sidebar` does.
- Order destinations by frequency: Sprint board, Backlog, Initiatives, Assistants.

### Content

- Keep labels to one or two words and pair each with its matching icon.

### Accessibility

- The icon is decorative; the visible `label` is the link's accessible name.
