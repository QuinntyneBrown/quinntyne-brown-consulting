# Border radii

Soft, slightly squared corners. Most components use one of two **role** radii — `--qbc-r-control` for anything you click
or type into, `--qbc-r-pill` for status pills and counts — and containers step up the scale with their size: rows at
`lg`, cards and columns at `xl`, the dialog at `2xl`.
