One row of the backlog list. `qbc-data-row` shows the `storyKey`, `title` and `context` (usually "Initiative / Epic") as the row's identity, followed by labelled columns filled through content projection.

Slots: `[state]` (status pill, priority tag), `[estimate]` (story points), `[owner]`, `[sprint]` (sprint select) and `[actions]` (buttons). Below 1050px the owner column hides; below 760px each row becomes a card with visible column labels.
