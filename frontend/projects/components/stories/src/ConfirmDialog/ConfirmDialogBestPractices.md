## Best practices

### Content

- Ask a direct question in the title ("Delete Sprint 14?").
- Use the copy to name the item and say what happens to the work that depends on it.
- Make the confirm label the verb ("Delete sprint"), never "OK" or "Yes".

### Accessibility

- Open it from a real button; focus returns to that trigger when the dialog closes.
- Reserve it for destructive or irreversible actions so it keeps its weight.
