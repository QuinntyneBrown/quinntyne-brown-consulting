A small rounded status label for sprints, stories, epics and assistants. `qbc-pill` renders `label` (or, when `label` is null, its projected content) capitalised in a coloured pill.

`tone` is a `PillTone` (default `muted`) that maps to one of four colour families: accent for ready/active/done/available, blue for draft/todo, amber for in-progress/planned/limited, and muted grey for archived/completed/unavailable.
