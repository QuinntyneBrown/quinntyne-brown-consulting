A coloured status pill for stories, initiatives and assistants. `qbc-status-pill` wraps `qbc-pill` as an inline block and passes through its two inputs.

`tone` is a `StatusPillTone` (default `muted`): `ready`, `active`, `done`, `available`, `draft`, `todo`/`toDo`, `progress`/`inProgress`, `planned`, `limited`, `archived`, `muted`, `unavailable` or `completed`. `label` sets the text; when it is `null` the projected content is shown instead.
