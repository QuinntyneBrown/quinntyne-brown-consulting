The QBC Workboard logo lockup: a square mark, the product name and a tagline, linked home. `qbc-brand` renders a router link labelled "QBC Workboard home".

Inputs are `mark` (default `Q`), `name` (default `QBC`), `tagline` (default `Workboard`) and `href` (default `/board`). It is usually projected into `qbc-sidebar` with the `brand` attribute.
