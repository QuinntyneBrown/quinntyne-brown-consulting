A summary card for one assistant on the team page. `qbc-assistant-card` shows a large avatar, the assistant's `name` and `role`, their `specialties` as tags, counts of assigned stories (`storyCount`) and open tasks (`openTaskCount`), and an availability indicator.

`availability` is `available` (default), `limited` or `unavailable`. Buttons projected with the `[actions]` attribute appear in the footer beside the availability indicator. An empty `specialties` list renders a "No specialties" tag.
