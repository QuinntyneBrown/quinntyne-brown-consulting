One row in a story's task list. `qbc-task-item` is a bordered grid with four columns (auto, flexible, 155px, auto) that lays out its projected content: typically a `qbc-checkbox`, a title `qbc-text-input`, an assignee `qbc-select` and a remove `qbc-button`.

`done` (default `false`) dims the row for completed tasks. Below 760px the grid drops to three columns.
