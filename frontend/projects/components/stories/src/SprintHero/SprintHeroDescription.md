The summary banner at the top of the board for the active sprint. `qbc-sprint-hero` shows an `eyebrow` (default "Current sprint"), the sprint `goal` as a heading, its `dates`, and a completion meter built from `complete` and `total` stories.

The percentage is computed and rounded for you and drives a `qbc-progress` bar labelled "Sprint completion". Project content with the `actions` attribute to place buttons under the meter.
