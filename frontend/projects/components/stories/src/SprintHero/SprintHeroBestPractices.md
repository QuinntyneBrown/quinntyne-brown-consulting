## Best practices

### Layout

- Use one hero per board, above the columns; it stacks to a single column below 760px.

### Content

- Write the goal as an outcome ("Ship the backlog import"), not a sprint number.
- Count stories, not story points, in `complete` and `total`; the copy says "stories complete".
- Keep actions to one primary sprint-level action.

### Accessibility

- The section is labelled "Current sprint summary" and the meter has its own label, so no extra ARIA is needed.
