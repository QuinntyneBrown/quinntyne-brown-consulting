## Best practices

### Layout

- Let the column set the width; the card fills it and breaks long titles instead of overflowing.

### Content

- Keep the title to the story outcome and put the epic or initiative in `context`.
- Estimate with story points; leave `points` `null` rather than `0` for unestimated stories.
- Keep footer actions to one compact button.

### Accessibility

- Dragging is pointer-only; always offer another way (e.g. a status menu) to move a story.
- The title is an `h3`; keep the board column heading one level above it.
