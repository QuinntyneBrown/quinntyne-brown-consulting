A story on the board or in the backlog. `qbc-story-card` shows the `storyKey` (default "QBC-000") with the story points (`points`, `null` for unestimated), the story `title`, a `context` line such as its epic or initiative, and a footer with the `owner`'s avatar and name ("Unassigned" when empty).

`draggableCard` makes the card draggable for moving it between board columns, and `dragging` dims it while it is in flight. Project buttons with the `actions` attribute into the footer.
