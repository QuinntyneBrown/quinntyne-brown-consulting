A placeholder for a page or list with nothing in it yet. `qbc-empty-state` shows an icon, a required `title` and one line of copy from `subtitle` or `description` (subtitle wins), followed by any buttons projected into `[actions]`.

`icon` takes any `IconName` and defaults to `empty`. `bare` removes the dashed frame and tightens the padding for use inside cards, lists and dialogs.
