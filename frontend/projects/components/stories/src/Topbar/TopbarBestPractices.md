## Best practices

### Layout

- Render one top bar per page, after the sidebar; it sticks to the top while the page scrolls.

### Content

- Keep the breadcrumb to "Workspace / Area".
- Limit actions to one or two, with the primary action last.

### Accessibility

- Bind `navOpen` to the sidebar's `open` state and update `menuLabel` to match ("Open"/"Close navigation").
